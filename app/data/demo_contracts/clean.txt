EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT (REFERENCE / SAMPLE)

Between:    Riyadh Care Services LLC (Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia)
And:        [REDACTED — domestic worker, Filipino citizen, Manila]

Date:       2026-04-12

1. POSITION
   Domestic Worker (live-in), Riyadh household.

2. TERM
   Two (2) years, renewable upon mutual written agreement.

3. SALARY
   Saudi Riyal (SAR) 2,200 per month, paid by bank transfer to the
   Employee's named account on or before the 5th of each Gregorian month.
   Salary may not be reduced during the contract term.

4. RECRUITMENT FEES
   All recruitment, placement, processing, visa, and travel costs are
   borne entirely by the Employer in accordance with the Fair Recruitment
   Initiative. No fees of any kind shall be charged to the Employee.

5. DEDUCTIONS
   Deductions from salary may only be made for purposes authorized by
   KSA Labor Law Art. 92, with the Employee's prior written consent for
   each instance. Total deductions shall not exceed 25% of monthly salary
   except as required by court order.

6. WORKING HOURS
   Eight (8) hours per day, six (6) days per week, with one paid weekly
   rest day of 24 consecutive hours (Friday).

7. OVERTIME
   Overtime is voluntary and paid at 1.5× the standard hourly rate.

8. HOUSING
   The Employer provides separate, lockable, private accommodation with
   24/7 free access to mobile phone, internet, and personal time.

9. ANNUAL LEAVE
   30 days of paid annual leave per year, plus statutory public holidays.

10. MEDICAL
    Comprehensive health insurance fully paid by Employer.

11. IDENTITY DOCUMENTS
    The Employee retains custody of her own passport and all personal
    identity documents at all times.

12. COMMUNICATIONS
    The Employee has unrestricted right to communicate with family, the
    embassy, and any third party using her own phone.

13. TERMINATION
    Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. Employer
    bears repatriation cost in all termination scenarios.

14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
    Disputes may be referred to the Saudi Labor Court and/or the
    Philippine Embassy POLO Riyadh. Both parties acknowledge the
    Employee's right to seek consular assistance at any time.
