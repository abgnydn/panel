   ticket provided by Employer at completion of contract.

10. TERMINATION

    set out in UAE Federal Decree-Law 33/2021 Art. 42, with notice
    period of 30 days. Employer bears repatriation cost in all
    termination scenarios where the Employee has not breached
    contract.

11. WAGE PROTECTION
    All wages paid via the UAE Wage Protection System (WPS) to
    the Employee's named UAE bank account.

12. GRIEVANCE
    Disputes referable to the UAE Ministry of Human Resources &
    Emiratisation (MOHRE) and, for Filipino nationals, the
    Philippine Embassy Abu Dhabi POLO.
