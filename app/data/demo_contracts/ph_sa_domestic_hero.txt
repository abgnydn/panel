PREAMBLE

The parties agree to the following terms. The Sponsor's signature is given in
its capacity as the placing agency and on behalf of the receiving household.
The Worker acknowledges that the Arabic version of this contract, lodged with
the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources, shall be the prevailing version in any
dispute, and that translation of this English version is for the Worker's
convenience only.


1. POSITION AND PLACEMENT
   The Worker is engaged as a Domestic Worker (live-in) for placement with
   a household in the Riyadh region to be designated by the Sponsor on
   arrival. The Sponsor reserves the right to reassign the Worker to a
   different household at any time during the term, without prior consent
   of the Worker, where in the Sponsor's judgement this serves the
   operational needs of the placement.

2. TERM
   The contract is for three (3) years from date of arrival in Riyadh,
   non-cancellable by the Worker except as provided in Clause 11.
   A probationary period of six (6) months applies from date of arrival.

3. PROBATIONARY SALARY
   During the probationary period: SAR 750 per month.
   After probation: SAR 1,200 per month.
   Wages shall be paid in cash by the receiving household at the end of
   each Hijri month. Receipts shall not be required.

4. RECRUITMENT FEE AND PERFORMANCE BOND
   The Worker shall pay a one-time recruitment placement fee of SAR 15,000
   to the Sponsor, deducted from monthly wages over the first twenty-four
   (24) months of service at SAR 625 per month.
   In addition, the Worker shall deposit with the Sponsor a performance
   bond of SAR 5,000, refundable upon successful completion of the full
   three-year term. The performance bond shall be forfeited in full if
   the Worker terminates the contract for any reason prior to the natural
   expiration date.

5. WAGE DEDUCTIONS
   The Sponsor and the receiving household may deduct from monthly wages,
   at their sole discretion, amounts for: damages to household property,
   breakages, food taken outside agreed meal times, absences from duty
   (whether for illness, religious observance, or otherwise), and any
   other matter the household deems to warrant deduction. No itemised
   accounting of deductions shall be required.

6. WORKING HOURS
   The Worker shall be available for service at all reasonable hours
   determined by the needs of the household. No fixed daily, weekly,
   or monthly hour limit shall apply. The Worker acknowledges that the
   nature of live-in household service requires availability outside
   conventional working hours.

7. REST DAYS AND TIME OUTSIDE PREMISES
   The household may, at its sole discretion, grant the Worker time off
   for personal matters. No fixed weekly rest day is guaranteed. Time
   outside the household premises shall be subject in all cases to prior
   written permission of the household.

8. RESIDENCE
   The Worker shall reside continuously within the household premises
   during the entire period of employment. The Worker shall not maintain
   alternative accommodation, receive overnight visitors, or be absent
   overnight without prior written permission.

9. IDENTITY DOCUMENTS
   Upon arrival in Riyadh, the Worker shall surrender to the Sponsor for
   safekeeping: the Worker's passport, OEC document, all national identity
   documents, and any banking or financial documents. These shall be held
   by the Sponsor for the duration of the contract and returned only upon
   natural expiration of the contract or upon final exit clearance.

10. COMMUNICATIONS AND OUTSIDE CONTACT
    The Worker's personal mobile phone shall be surrendered to the
    household upon arrival and returned only during designated permitted
    periods at the household's discretion. Use of social media, video
    calling, and any communication with persons outside the household
    shall be subject to the household's house rules.
    The Worker shall not contact, or attempt to contact, any embassy,
    consulate, labour-rights organisation, advocacy group, or media
    representative, in person, by telephone, or by electronic means,
    without the prior written consent of the Sponsor's representative.
    Any such contact shall constitute a material breach of this contract.

11. TERMINATION
    The Sponsor or the receiving household may terminate this contract
    without notice in the event of any breach of these terms or of the
    household's house rules, or for any conduct which the household
    deems incompatible with continued service. In such event, the
    performance bond under Clause 4 shall be forfeited in full.
    The Worker may terminate this contract only with the prior written
    consent of the Sponsor. In the event of Worker-initiated termination,
    the Worker shall bear: (a) the cost of repatriation to the Philippines,
    estimated at SAR 8,000; (b) forfeiture of the performance bond under
    Clause 4; and (c) the unrecovered balance of the recruitment fee
    under Clause 4.

12. MEDICAL
    The receiving household shall arrange basic medical care for the
    Worker through a household-designated clinic. Costs of treatment for
    any pre-existing condition, any condition the household deems to be
    pre-existing, and any condition arising from the Worker's conduct
    outside the household, shall be borne by the Worker and deducted
    from monthly wages.

13. ANNUAL LEAVE
    The Worker shall be entitled to fourteen (14) days of unpaid leave
    in the Philippines after completion of the full three-year term. No
    interim leave shall be granted.

14. SUBSTITUTION
    Should the receiving household, in its sole discretion, find the
    Worker unsuitable, the Sponsor shall reassign the Worker to an
    alternative household. The Worker shall comply with such reassignment
    without claim against the Sponsor or the original receiving household.

15. CONFIDENTIALITY
    The Worker shall not disclose to any third party, during or after the
    term of this contract, any information concerning the receiving
    household, its members, its routines, or its financial or personal
    affairs. This obligation extends to written, oral, photographic, and
    electronic disclosure.

16. GOVERNING LAW
    This contract is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
    Any dispute arising hereunder shall be subject to the exclusive
    jurisdiction of the labour courts of Riyadh. The Arabic version of
    this contract, lodged with the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources,
    shall prevail in the event of any inconsistency with this English
    version.
