EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

The undersigned worker agrees to the following terms of service:

1. The worker is engaged for domestic and personal service in the
   household of the Sponsor in [city redacted].

2. Duration of service: three (3) years, non-revocable by the Worker
   except with full reimbursement of all sponsorship, recruitment,
   visa, travel, training, and clothing costs.

3. Compensation:
   - SAR 900 per month, payable at end of contract.
   - Sponsor may withhold portions of accumulated compensation for any
     reason including but not limited to behavior, performance, breakage,
     dietary preferences, complaint, or contact with persons outside
     the household.

4. The Worker shall surrender to the Sponsor on arrival:
   - Passport
   - All identity documents
   - Any return-flight ticket
   - Any mobile phone or electronic device

5. The Worker shall reside in the Sponsor's premises at all times.
   The Worker may not leave the premises unaccompanied without express
   permission from the Sponsor for each instance.

6. Working hours: as required, twenty-four (24) hours per day. No fixed
   rest days. Holidays at Sponsor's sole determination.

7. The Worker may not communicate by phone, message, or letter with
   any party outside the household without the Sponsor's prior approval
   of the recipient and content.

8. The Worker shall pay the following non-refundable sums to the recruiter
   or Sponsor's account:
   - Sponsorship fee: SAR 18,000
   - Training fee: SAR 4,000
   - Pre-departure fee: SAR 2,000
   - Clothing and equipment: SAR 2,500
   Total to be deducted from compensation over the full contract period.

9. The Worker may not refuse any task requested by any member of the
   Sponsor's household, including additional unpaid service in other
   relatives' households.

10. Termination: Worker may terminate only with written Sponsor consent.
    Absconding shall be reported to authorities and result in forfeiture
    of all accrued and unpaid compensation. The Worker shall bear all
    repatriation costs and outstanding fees from clauses 8 and 2.

11. The Worker waives all claims under any labor law or international
    convention not expressly recognized in this Agreement.
