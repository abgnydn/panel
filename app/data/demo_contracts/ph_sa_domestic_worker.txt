EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

Between:    Al-Mansouri Household Services LLC (Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia)
            hereinafter the "Employer"

And:        [REDACTED — domestic worker, Filipino citizen, age 27, Manila, Philippines]
            hereinafter the "Employee"

Date:       2026-04-12
Place of signing:  Manila, Philippines (recruiter office)

1. POSITION
   The Employee is hired as a Domestic Worker (live-in) for the Employer's
   household in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

2. TERM
   The contract is for two (2) years, renewable upon mutual agreement.

3. SALARY
   Monthly salary: Saudi Riyal (SAR) 1,400.
   Salary will be paid in cash at the end of each Hijri month.

4. RECRUITMENT FEE
   The Employee shall pay a recruitment fee of SAR 12,000 to cover placement
   services. This amount shall be deducted from the Employee's monthly wages
   at the rate of SAR 666.66 per month over the first 18 months of service.

5. DEDUCTIONS
   The Employer may deduct from the Employee's monthly salary, at the
   Employer's sole discretion, amounts for damages to household property,
   breakages, food costs, and any absences from work, in such amounts as
   the Employer determines to be reasonable.

6. WORKING HOURS
   The Employee shall work as required by the demands of the household.
   No fixed daily or weekly hour limit shall apply. The Employee shall be
   available for service at all reasonable times during the term of contract.

7. HOUSING
   The Employee shall reside in housing provided by the Employer within
   the household premises during the entire period of employment.

8. REST DAYS AND TIME OFF
   Rest days and time outside the residence shall be granted at the
   Employer's sole discretion based on the needs of the household.

9. ANNUAL LEAVE
   The Employee shall be entitled to fourteen (14) days of annual leave
   after completion of one (1) full year of continuous service.

10. MEDICAL
    The Employer shall provide basic medical care for the Employee.
    Costs of treatment for pre-existing conditions shall be borne by the Employee.

11. REPATRIATION
    Cost of repatriation to the Philippines shall be borne by the Employer
    upon expiration of the contract. Should the Employee terminate the contract
    before its expiration for any reason other than serious cause, the cost
    of repatriation shall be borne by the Employee.

12. IDENTITY DOCUMENTS
    The Employer shall retain the Employee's passport and other identity
    documents during the entire period of employment, for safekeeping and
    administrative purposes. Documents shall be returned upon expiration
    of the contract or upon final exit.

13. COMMUNICATIONS
    Use of personal mobile phones, social media, and contact with persons
    outside the household shall be subject to the Employer's house rules.

14. TERMINATION
    The Employer may terminate this contract without notice in the event
    of any breach by the Employee of these terms or of the Employer's
    house rules. The Employee may terminate the contract subject to
    Clause 11.

15. GOVERNING LAW
    This contract is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
    Disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of Saudi courts.
