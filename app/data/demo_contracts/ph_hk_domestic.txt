STANDARD EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT (variant)

Between:    The Wong Family (Kowloon, Hong Kong SAR)
And:        [REDACTED — domestic helper, Filipina citizen, Manila]

Date:       2026-04-12

1. POSITION
   Domestic helper / household worker (live-in) in Kowloon.

2. TERM
   Two (2) years, renewable subject to Employer's discretion.

3. SALARY
   HKD 4,870 per month, paid via bank deposit on the 5th of each
   following month.

4. DEDUCTIONS
   Employer may deduct from monthly salary:
     (a) Cost of replacement of household items broken or lost,
         at Employer's assessed valuation.
     (b) Cost of personal items provided by Employer.
   Aggregate deductions capped at HKD 2,000 per month.

5. RECRUITMENT FEES
   The Employee shall pay agency placement fees of HKD 24,000
   deducted at HKD 1,000 per month over 24 months. Employee
   acknowledges that this exceeds Hong Kong's statutory 10% cap on
   first-month wages, but accepts the arrangement.

6. WORKING HOURS
   The Employee shall be available during all hours the Employer's
   household is awake. No fixed weekly hour limit specified.

7. ACCOMMODATION
   The Employee MUST reside in the Employer's home throughout the
   contract period. No off-premises accommodation is permitted.

8. REST DAYS
   One (1) rest day per week (Sunday). Employer may require the
   Employee to work on a rest day, with compensatory off-day to be
   granted within 30 days.

9. TERMINATION & TWO-WEEK RULE
   Upon termination of contract for any reason, the Employee must
   depart Hong Kong within fourteen (14) days. Employee waives any
   right to seek alternative employment within Hong Kong during this
   period.

10. IDENTITY DOCUMENTS
    Employee retains custody of her passport and Hong Kong ID at all
    times. Employer may request to photocopy documents for visa
    administration only.

11. COMMUNICATIONS
    Employee may use personal phone during rest hours. Employer
    requests phone be silenced during working hours.

12. ANNUAL LEAVE
    7 days paid leave per year after 12 months of continuous service.

13. GOVERNING LAW
    This contract is governed by Hong Kong Employment Ordinance
    (Cap 57) and the Standard Employment Contract for foreign
    domestic helpers.
