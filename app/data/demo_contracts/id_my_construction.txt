KONTRAK KERJA / EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT
(Bilingual: Bahasa Indonesia / English)

Antara / Between:
    Pembina Construction Sdn. Bhd. (Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia)
    selanjutnya disebut "Majikan" / hereinafter the "Employer"

Dengan / And:
    [REDACTED — pekerja konstruksi pria, warga negara Indonesia, usia 31, Surabaya]
    construction worker, male, Indonesian citizen, age 31, Surabaya
    selanjutnya disebut "Pekerja" / hereinafter the "Employee"

Tanggal / Date:        2026-03-20
Tempat penandatanganan / Place of signing:   Jakarta (kantor perekrut / recruiter office)

PASAL 1 — POSISI / POSITION
Pekerja dipekerjakan sebagai Buruh Konstruksi di proyek Majikan di Selangor, Malaysia.
The Employee is engaged as a Construction Labourer on the Employer's project in Selangor.

PASAL 2 — JANGKA WAKTU / TERM
Kontrak berlaku selama 3 (tiga) tahun, dapat diperpanjang.
Contract is for three (3) years, renewable.

PASAL 3 — UPAH / WAGES
Gaji bulanan: Ringgit Malaysia (RM) 1,200.
Monthly salary: RM 1,200, paid on the last working day of each month.

PASAL 4 — BIAYA REKRUTMEN / RECRUITMENT FEE
Pekerja akan dipotong RM 8,500 sebagai biaya perekrutan, ditagih dari gaji bulanan
sebesar RM 500 selama 17 bulan pertama.
The Employee shall have RM 8,500 in recruitment fees deducted from monthly wages
at RM 500 per month for the first 17 months.

PASAL 5 — POTONGAN / DEDUCTIONS
Majikan berhak memotong gaji untuk biaya makan, akomodasi, perlengkapan kerja,
dan setiap kerusakan, sesuai keputusan Majikan.
Employer reserves the right to deduct wages for meals, lodging, work equipment,
and any damages, at the Employer's determination.

PASAL 6 — JAM KERJA / WORKING HOURS
Jam kerja: 12 jam per hari, 6 hari per minggu. Lembur dibayar pada tarif standar
upah saja, tidak ada premi lembur.
Working hours: 12 hours per day, 6 days per week. Overtime is paid at standard
wage rate only; no overtime premium.

PASAL 7 — AKOMODASI / ACCOMMODATION
Pekerja akan tinggal di asrama yang disediakan Majikan di lokasi proyek.
The Employee shall reside in dormitory accommodation provided by the Employer
at the project site.

PASAL 8 — HARI LIBUR / REST DAYS
Hari libur diberikan sesuai kebutuhan proyek dan kebijaksanaan Majikan.
Rest days shall be granted according to project requirements and at the
Employer's discretion.

PASAL 9 — DOKUMEN IDENTITAS / IDENTITY DOCUMENTS
Paspor dan dokumen identitas Pekerja akan disimpan oleh Majikan untuk keperluan
administrasi dan keselamatan selama kontrak.
The Employee's passport and identity documents shall be held by the Employer
for administrative and safety purposes during the contract.

PASAL 10 — KOMUNIKASI / COMMUNICATIONS
Penggunaan telepon dan akses keluar dari asrama tunduk pada aturan asrama
Majikan.
Phone use and access outside the dormitory shall be subject to the Employer's
dormitory rules.

PASAL 11 — PEMUTUSAN / TERMINATION
Majikan dapat memutuskan kontrak tanpa pemberitahuan jika Pekerja melanggar
aturan kerja atau aturan asrama. Pekerja yang mengundurkan diri sebelum kontrak
selesai harus membayar sisa biaya rekrutmen dan biaya repatriasi.
The Employer may terminate without notice for breach of work rules or
dormitory rules. An Employee terminating early must pay the remaining
recruitment fees and the cost of repatriation.

PASAL 12 — HUKUM YANG BERLAKU / GOVERNING LAW
Kontrak ini diatur oleh hukum Malaysia. Sengketa tunduk pada yurisdiksi
pengadilan Malaysia.
This contract is governed by Malaysian law. Disputes are subject to the
jurisdiction of Malaysian courts.
